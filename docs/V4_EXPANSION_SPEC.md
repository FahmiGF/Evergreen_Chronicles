# EVERGREEN CHRONICLES — V4 ULTIMATE EXPANSION SPECIFICATION

## Overview
Evergreen Chronicles Version 4.0.0 is the definitive expansion advancing the game from a cozy farming RPG into an interconnected fantasy life-simulation, exploration, and civilization sandbox.

---

## 1. Player Progression & 12-Skill Tree Engine
- **12 Skills**: `farming`, `mining`, `fishing`, `foraging`, `crafting`, `combat`, `cooking`, `social`, `exploration`, `animal_care`, `building`, `magic`.
- **Leveling Curve**: Non-linear exponential XP curve (`XP = 100 * (level - 1)^1.65`).
- **Milestone Tiers**: Beginner (1-10), Apprentice (11-20), Skilled (21-30), Expert (31-40), Master (41-50), Grandmaster (51-75), Legendary (76-100).
- **Branching Perk Trees**: Each skill features 3 unique branches with 5 progressive unlockable perks (e.g., Crop Mastery, Land Mastery, Nature Harmony for Farming; Vein Breaker, Geologist, Deep Delver for Mining).
- **Prestige System**: Skills reaching Level 50+ can unlock Prestige ranks, prestige badges, and grandmaster titles without wiping inventory or progression.

---

## 2. Tools & Equipment Progression
- **10 Tool Tiers**: Wooden -> Copper -> Iron -> Silver -> Gold -> Platinum -> Mythril -> Crystal -> Ancient -> Celestial.
- **Durability System**: Dynamic decay upon use, blacksmith repairs using coins, and tier upgrades increasing max durability and stamina efficiency.
- **Magical Enchantments**: Socketable upgrades (`Fortune`, `Swift Strike`, `Auto-Smelt`, `Multi-Tile`, `Radiance`) infused using Aether Shards.

---

## 3. Mounts & Stables System
- **10 Mounts**: Horse, Pony, Deer, Giant Rabbit, Alpaca, Wolf Mount, Crystal Fox, Unicorn, Griffin, Dragon.
- **Speed Multipliers**: Walk speed bonus ranging from +40% (Pony) to +175% (Dragon).
- **Bonding Activities**: Feeding, Grooming, Playing, and Training with live happiness and stamina bars.
- **Stables**: 6 Tiers (Small Stable to Celestial Sanctuary) expanding mount capacity from 2 up to 12.

---

## 4. Underground Mining & Machinery
- **10 Zones / 60+ Floors**: From Abandoned Quarry down to the Celestial Mine.
- **Mineral Catalog**: 13 minerals, rough gemstones, and rare cores.
- **Machinery Processing**: Blast Smelter (ore to ingots), Ore Crusher (geodes to crystals), Gem Polisher (faceted royal gems), and automated minecart routing.

---

## 5. Ocean Fishing & Boats
- **7 Water Zones**: Peaceful Lake, River Valley, Coastal Beach, Coral Reef, Deep Ocean, Sunken Kingdom, Frozen Sea.
- **Boats**: 5 tiers (Wooden Raft, Fishing Dinghy, Sailing Sloop, Steam Exploration Vessel, Arcane Skiff).
- **Aquarium Journal**: Fish specimen length tracking, quality star evaluation (Normal, Silver, Gold, Iridium), and village aquarium collection.

---

## 6. Pets & Animal Ranch
- **10 Companions**: Cat, Dog, Bunny, Fox, Owl, Turtle, Tiny Dragon, Lumina Spirit, Slime, Crystal Butterfly with active companion traits.
- **Ranch Husbandry**: Chicken Coop, Cow Barn, Alpaca Sanctuary, and Apiary with daily animal harvests.

---

## 7. Buildings & Industrial Automation
- **13 Production Buildings**: Greenhouse, Bakery, Dairy, Winery/Juice House, Textile Workshop, Carpenter Workshop, Blacksmith, Alchemy Lab, Gem Workshop, Automated Factory, Research Center, Trading Post, Warehouse.
- **Automation Grid**: Sprinklers, conveyor routing, and asynchronous processing queues.

---

## 8. Timed Portals & Halloween Madness Seasonal Event
- **8 Timed Portals**: Forest, Crystal, Sky, Ocean, Shadow, Ancient, Seasonal, and Celestial with real-time countdown clocks and dimensional relic drops.
- **Halloween Madness**:
  - Whimsical village decoration with glowing Jack-o'-lanterns.
  - Ghost Pumpkin farming (`seed_ghost_pumpkin` -> `crop_ghost_pumpkin`).
  - Trick-or-Treat visits with town NPCs granting candies and Spooky Tokens.
  - Event Shop with exclusive seasonal vanity cosmetics (Witch Hat, Vampire Cape, Ghost Lantern, Skeletal Mount Saddle).

---

## 9. Master Collection Journal & Guilds
- **Encyclopedia**: Centralized catalog of crops, minerals, fish with discovery completion tracking.
- **Achievements**: 25+ milestone goals with claimable gold rewards.
- **7 Guilds**: Farmer, Miner, Explorer, Fisher, Merchant, Builder, Adventurer Guilds with passive guild perks.

# CHANGELOG — VERSION 4.0.0 (ULTIMATE EXPANSION UPDATE)

## Major Systems Added

### 1. 12-Skill Progression & Branching Perk Trees
- Added 12 complete skill paths: Farming, Mining, Fishing, Foraging, Crafting, Combat, Cooking, Social, Exploration, Animal Care, Building, Magic.
- Added milestone badges from Beginner (1-10) to Legendary (76-100).
- Added branching skill trees (3 specialized branches per skill, 5 progressive perks per branch).
- Added Prestige System with non-destructive prestige badges and titles for mastery players.

### 2. 10 Tool Tiers & Equipment Engine
- Added 10 tiers of tools: Wooden, Copper, Iron, Silver, Gold, Platinum, Mythril, Crystal, Ancient, Celestial.
- Added durability decay and repair mechanics at Blacksmith.
- Added tool enchantments: Fortune, Swift Strike, Auto-Smelt, Multi-Tile, Radiance.

### 3. Mounts & Stables System
- Added 10 mount species: Horse, Pony, Deer, Giant Rabbit, Alpaca, Wolf, Crystal Fox, Unicorn, Griffin, Dragon.
- Added stable renovations (Small Stable to Celestial Sanctuary).
- Added bonding actions: Feed, Groom, Play, Train.
- Integrated mount rendering under player on 2D canvas with +40% to +175% movement speed boost.

### 4. Underground Mining & Processing Machinery
- Added 10 underground zones covering 60 floors with elevator quick-travel checkpoints.
- Added Blast Smelter, Ore Crusher, and Gem Polisher machinery.
- Added 13-item mineral catalog including rough gems, Mythril, and Ancient Cores.

### 5. Ocean Fishing & Boat Armada
- Added 7 ocean/water zones and 5 boat tiers (Wooden Raft to Arcane Skiff).
- Added dynamic fish sizes, rarity evaluation, and quality ratings (Normal, Silver, Gold, Iridium).
- Added Village Aquarium collection records.

### 6. Companions & Animal Ranching
- Added 10 companion pets with active passive traits (e.g. Treasure Sniffer, Cozy Purr).
- Added ranch husbandry for Chicken Coops, Cow Barns, Alpaca Sanctuaries, and Apiaries.

### 7. Buildings & Industrial Automation
- Added 13 functional buildings and production queues.
- Added automated sprinklers and conveyor sorting pipelines.

### 8. Timed Dimensional Portals & Halloween Madness
- Added 8 timed dimensional portals with real-time countdown clocks.
- Added Halloween Madness event with spooky village decor, Ghost Pumpkin farming, NPC Trick-or-Treating, and Spooky Vanity Shop.

### 9. Master Collection Journal & Guilds
- Centralized encyclopedia tracking discovery % for crops, minerals, and fish.
- Added 25+ achievements with claimable rewards.
- Added 7 player guilds with passive guild perks.

### 10. Modern Responsive UI Modals
- `SkillsProgressionModal.jsx` [Hotkey: K]
- `MountsStableModal.jsx` [Hotkey: R]
- `UndergroundMiningModal.jsx` [Hotkey: M]
